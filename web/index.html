ARSENAL

VULCAN

1 dmg rapid-fire nose cannon. Reliable baseline suppression when your Wave Cannon is uncharged.

WAVE CANNON

8 / 20 / 48 dmg across three charge tiers. Pierces formations cleanly; you cannot fire vulcan while charging.

FORCE POD

Indestructible. Attach front or rear by contact to shield your hull and fire your laser backwards; launch it as a 4-dmg ram; detached, it mirrors your height and fires L1 forward / L2 V / L3 K patterns. 3 levels via crystals.

RED CRYSTAL

Twin forward counter-oscillating lasers that weave into a concentrated helix beam at max level.

BLUE CRYSTAL

3 simultaneous beams (forward and ±45° diagonals) that reflect off solid terrain up to twice.

YELLOW CRYSTAL

Up/down vertical flame plumes that lock onto ceiling and floor surfaces and crawl along terrain.

BITS

Up to 2 orbiting satellite pods (≤2) that block incoming bullets and fire extra forward shots with the red laser.

MISSILES

Twin homing ordnance (2×3 dmg) that seeks armoured ground turrets and airborne threats independently.

SPEED

Thruster upgrades capped at level 3, scaling ship velocity cleanly along 80 + 16 × level px/s.

SURVIVAL RULES

  • Persistent Hi-Score: Your highest score is saved automatically in your browser between sessions.
  • Three Lives & Checkpoints: Every run begins with 3 lives, with a mid-stage checkpoint at 60% of each stage's scroll.
  • Recovery After Death: Respawning keeps your Force pod at level 1 so you are never defenceless, but strips active crystals, bits, missiles, and speed-ups.
  • Unlimited Continues: Continuing resets your current run score to zero, never your recorded hi-score.
  • Supply Cadence: Carrier drones enter every 12 s and guarantee a power-up drop when destroyed.

PILOT TIPS

  • 01 // SHIELD WITH THE FORCE

    Dock the Force on your nose to absorb enemy bullets cleanly, or launch it forward into armoured targets as an indestructible 4-dmg battering ram.

  • 02 // REAR-ATTACH FOR THE BATTLESHIP

    On Stage 3 (Vesper Broadside), fly ahead of the Iron Vesper and dock the Force on your tail so your lasers fire straight backwards into its rear engine bay.

  • 03 // FULL CHARGE DELETES FORMATIONS

    Pre-charge a 1.8 s Wave Cannon before heavy waves arrive — a single 48-dmg beam pierces entire columns before they can return fire.

  • 04 // BLUE LASER OWNS CORRIDORS

    Blue crystal beams split at ±45° and ricochet twice off ceilings and floors, saturating tight biomechanical passages around blind corners.

  • 05 // YELLOW CLEARS GROUND LANES

    Yellow plumes climb vertical walls and hug ceilings and floors, sweeping entrenched turrets and crawlers without exposing your hull.

  • 06 // LEARN THE PATTERNS

    Rift Vector is deliberate, not twitch. Read the ≥0.6 s telegraphs, claim safe lanes early, and position your Force before weak points open.

ABOUT & CREDITS

Rift Vector is an original IP inspired by R-Type (Irem, 1987) — no Irem artwork, audio, names, or code are used.

Built with Defold 1.14.1 through a deterministic asset and verification pipeline: 48 palette-locked sprite clips, 22 mastered FM/PSG audio cues, a 50-check blind behavioural test suite, and a native Linux build shipping Vulkan + OpenGL shader parity.
